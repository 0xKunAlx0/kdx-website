// Case Studies
Why Organizations Need This
Real scenarios where offensive security prevents catastrophic breaches.
Scenario: Fintech Platform

IDOR in Payment API Exposes Customer Financial Data

A fintech startup's API allowed any authenticated user to access other users' transaction histories by manipulating account IDs. A VAPT engagement identified the broken object-level authorization before launch, preventing a potential data breach affecting thousands of users and regulatory penalties under DPDP Act.

✓ Identified pre-launch via VAPT
Scenario: Enterprise Network

Red Team Exercise Reveals Domain Admin in 4 Hours

A phishing email delivered during a red team engagement led to initial access. From there, lateral movement through misconfigured AD trusts achieved domain admin privileges within hours — bypassing EDR, IDS, and the SOC team. The exercise exposed critical gaps in detection and response capabilities.

✓ Detection gaps patched post-engagement
Scenario: Healthcare SaaS

Broken Authentication Chain Leads to Patient Record Access

A web application penetration test on a healthcare platform discovered a session fixation vulnerability chained with a privilege escalation bug, allowing access to patient medical records. The finding was classified as critical under CERT-In reporting requirements and remediated within 48 hours.

✓ Critical chain fixed within 48 hours
Scenario: E-Commerce

Cloud Storage Misconfiguration Leaks 200K User Records

A cloud security assessment revealed a publicly accessible S3 bucket containing customer PII, payment tokens, and internal credentials. The exposure had existed for months without detection. Our assessment included full enumeration, impact analysis, and guided remediation with AWS-native security controls.

✓ Exposure contained and secured
Scenario: Banking App

Mobile App Binary Leaks API Keys and Hardcoded Secrets

Reverse engineering of an Android banking application revealed hardcoded API keys, unencrypted local storage of session tokens, and disabled certificate pinning — allowing complete interception and manipulation of banking transactions via a MITM position.

✓ Remediated before production release
Scenario: Corporate Security

50% of Employees Fall for Targeted Phishing Campaign

A phishing simulation targeting 500 employees using custom-crafted pretexts achieved a 50% click rate and 30% credential submission rate. Results drove an overhaul of the company's security awareness program, reducing susceptibility to under 5% in follow-up campaigns.

✓ Awareness reduced click rate by 90%
// Regulatory Compliance
Indian Regulatory Framework
We help organizations meet India's cybersecurity compliance requirements through assessment, gap analysis, and remediation advisory.

IT Act, 2000

India's primary cyber law governing electronic governance, digital signatures, cybercrime, and data protection. Section 43A mandates reasonable security practices for handling sensitive personal data.

Mandatory

CERT-In Directions 2022

Mandates 6-hour incident reporting, 180-day log retention, synchronized system clocks, and KYC for VPN/cloud providers. Non-compliance attracts penalties under the IT Act.

Mandatory

DPDP Act, 2023

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act establishes consent-based data processing, data fiduciary obligations, breach notification requirements, and significant penalties up to ₹250 crore.

Mandatory

RBI Cybersecurity Framework

RBI guidelines for banks and NBFCs covering cyber resilience, SOC operations, red team testing, and IT risk management. Periodic VAPT is mandatory for regulated entities.

Financial Sector

SEBI CSCRF

SEBI's Cyber Security and Cyber Resilience Framework for stock exchanges, depositories, and market intermediaries. Mandates periodic vulnerability assessment and penetration testing.

Capital Markets

IRDAI Guidelines

Information and Cybersecurity Guidelines for insurers covering data classification, access controls, incident management, and mandatory annual VAPT assessments.
